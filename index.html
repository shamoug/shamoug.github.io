Working software that reasons on your own machine. No cloud, no account, no telemetry.

Mandata

Windows, macOS, Linux, with an Android companion

A desktop companion for Microsoft Outlook that reads your email threads, finds the tasks hiding inside them, and follows each one to closure. It records who asked whom for what and by when, drafts the follow-ups, answers plain-language questions with cited sources, and compiles weekly reports. Every inference runs locally through Ollama, so your mail never leaves your machine.

Finad

Windows desktop, Android alerts

A market intelligence engine that reads twenty news feeds, maps every story to the companies and macro themes it touches, and weighs the result against each stock's position in its 52-week range. The output is a disciplined signal: sell strength meeting bad news, buy weakness meeting good news, and the conviction behind each call. Signals are algorithmic and informational, not investment advice.

SDG Media Monitor

Live in the browser

A live observatory of how the world's press covers the Sustainable Development Goals. Articles are gathered, summarized in several languages, and plotted on an interactive world map, with time windows from a single day to the full archive and filters by goal, region, and theme.

The Parlor

Installable web app, works offline

Eight evidence-informed word games for speech and language therapy, built for people with aphasia, apraxia, dysarthria, articulation disorders, and stuttering, and for the clinicians and families who support them. Every game ends in spoken practice, every attempt is logged for progress review, and every record stays in the browser. Free and private by design.

Common Ground

Browser, solo or multiplayer

Five games about the same work: a UN Country Team holding a crisis together. A cinematic board race through lucky breaks and field crises, a tower defense, a shared word duel for two officers, a diplomatic chess variant where captured pieces are won over rather than lost, and a Global Goals quiz show that turns phones into private answer pads.

Save the Date

Browser, online multiplayer

A systems-thinking game about acting under uncertainty. Two towns share one river and one approaching flood; every round the forecast sharpens while the power to act drains away. Act early and risk a false alarm, wait for certainty and it is already too late. Built for workshops, it runs itself with no facilitator.

Ghost Buster GO

Phone, installable web app

An augmented-reality ghost hunt played on real streets. A live map spawns deterministic ghosts around you, so every player standing in the same place sees the same spirits; the rear camera and a hold-to-fire lightning stream do the rest. Crews, a tuned boss fight, and a Ghostdex of eight original species, with no app store and no server to run.

SEmHuS

Published research, Journal of International Humanitarian Action

A semantically embedded humanitarian space: doctoral research that applies AI/NLP and word embeddings to humanitarian corpora, so that the language of crisis response can be mapped, measured, and queried. The repository holds the trained model, tokenizer, and analysis notebooks behind the published article.

Ollama Sandbox

Python command line

A minimal command-line tool for conversing with local language models through Ollama, preserving conversational context across turns. A small utility, and the seed of the local-first approach that Mandata and Finad grew from.
